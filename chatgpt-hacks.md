# Useful ChatGPT Prompt Codes & Learning Hacks

## Writing & Communication

### `/human` (or `Humanize`)

Forces ChatGPT to write in a natural, conversational, and less robotic tone by reducing excessive AI-style wording.

**Example:**
`/human Explain GenAI`

---

### `ELI5` (Explain Like I’m 5)

Breaks down difficult or technical concepts into very simple language that anyone can understand.

**Example:**
`ELI5 What is machine learning?`

---

### `Jargonize`

Converts simple text into polished, professional, business, or academic language.

**Example:**
`Jargonize: Our app helps people learn English`

---

### `TL;DR`

Creates a short summary of long text, articles, reports, or explanations.

**Example:**
`TL;DR this article`

---

### `/expand`

Tells ChatGPT to go deeper and provide more details, examples, and explanations.

**Example:**
`/expand Explain GenAI`

---

### `ALT3`

Requests three different versions or variations of an answer.

**Example:**
`ALT3 I am Asabeneh Yetayeh and introduce me to the world`

---

## Critical Thinking & Honest Feedback

### `/truthmode` (or `Kill Critic`)

Forces ChatGPT to stop overly agreeing and instead provide honest, direct, and critical feedback.

**Example:**
`/truthmode Review my startup idea`

---

### `REDTEAM`

Makes the AI challenge your idea by acting as a devil’s advocate and identifying weaknesses, risks, or blind spots.

**Example:**
`REDTEAM Analyze my business plan`

---

### `FUTUREYOU`

Prompts ChatGPT to act like a wiser future version of yourself and give guidance or advice.

**Example:**
`FUTUREYOU What should I focus on this year?`

---

## Learning & Understanding

### `Feynman Technique`

A powerful learning method with four steps:

1. **Simplify** the topic
2. **Explain** it clearly
3. **Review** gaps in understanding
4. **Refine** and improve the explanation

Great for mastering difficult concepts.

---

### `PARETO`

Focuses on the 20% of information that delivers 80% of the results.

**Example:**
`PARETO JavaScript for web development`

---

### `X10 Think`

Encourages deeper reasoning and more thoughtful problem-solving.

**Example:**
`X10 Think How can I scale my learning platform?`

---

## Coding & Development

### `/code_review`

Makes ChatGPT act like a senior software engineer to review your code and suggest improvements, optimizations, and best practices.

**Example:**
`/code_review Review this React component`

---

### `/vs`

Generates a side-by-side comparison between two tools, technologies, frameworks, or ideas.

**Example:**
`/vs Sweden vs Finland`

---

## Persona & Role-Playing Prompts

### `/ACT AS: [role]`

Instantly transforms ChatGPT into a specific expert role or professional persona.

**Examples:**

* `/ACT AS: UX Researcher`
* `/ACT AS: SEO Expert`
* `/ACT AS: Senior Software Engineer`
* `/ACT AS: Startup Mentor`

---

# Permanent Custom Instructions

You can improve ChatGPT’s responses permanently using Custom Instructions.

### Steps:

1. Open **Settings**
2. Go to **Custom Instructions**
3. Under **“What would you like ChatGPT to know about you?”**, add your preferences.

**Example Preferences:**

* “Always use a conversational tone.”
* “Explain concepts step by step.”
* “Use simple English.”
* “Provide code examples when possible.”

---

# Pro Tip

You can combine multiple prompt codes together for even better results.

**Example:**
`/human + ELI5 + /expand Explain GenAI`

This will:

* Use natural language
* Keep explanations simple
* Provide detailed information
